🧪 Understanding Soil & Climatic Parameters

🧪 Nitrogen (N)

Essential for leaf growth, chlorophyll development, and vegetative strength in cereal crops like Rice and Maize.

⚡ Phosphorus (P)

Crucial for root development, seed production, and early plant vigor. High in legumes and pulses like Chickpea.

💎 Potassium (K)

Regulates water retention, disease resistance, and fruit quality. Critical for heavy potassium feeders like Apple and Grapes.

🌡️ Temperature (°C)

Governs metabolic rates and photosynthesis. Distinguishes cool temperate crops (Apples) from tropical crops (Papaya).

💧 Relative Humidity (%)

Impacts transpiration and fungal susceptibility. Essential factor for humid coastal crops like Coconut and Jute.

🌧️ Annual Rainfall (mm)

The #1 predictor feature in our model! Determines total water availability for high-water crops (Rice, Jute) vs dryland crops (Mothbeans).
